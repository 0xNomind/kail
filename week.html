A week in a minute

Ten boats the computer plays go through seven days of Kail, here in your browser: the rules they play by on NEAR, the real sea, and a model of the contract's rules. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing here is on the chain.

The week

    Press “Play the week”. Each day takes about eight seconds.

    While it plays, this line shows what one boat after another decides, in the boat's own words.

    How it moved

    Day by day

    Prize: what a full draw could pay that day, a fifth of the prize money when the day began; a winning ticket gets a hundredth of it. Earned: tickets from quests. Fish: how many changed hands.

    The boats

    NEAR: what a boat has more or less than it started with, counting what it is owed.

    What the boats decided, and why

    • The boats' decisions are listed here as the week goes, newest first.

    The books

    After every day everything is added up: NEAR, coins, tickets, fish. A tick under a day means nothing appeared or vanished without a rule for it.

    The same week from the command line: npm run simulate. The model is src/model.js, the boats' rules are src/npc-rules.js, and npm run verify checks the model against the contract on NEAR.
