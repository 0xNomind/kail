Your NEAR

You signed in with Google or email, so there is no wallet app: this is your account on NEAR, and its NEAR is yours. The name was made for you at your first sign-in and stays as it is: an account on NEAR cannot be renamed. It pays for top-ups and for the small fee of every action (about 0.0003 NEAR). Keep at least 0.02 NEAR in it: about that much has to be at hand while an action runs.

What you approve in NEAR Auth's window (a top-up, buying tickets) costs 0.005 NEAR on top: your key is kept by NEAR's network, and each signature it gives has a fee. Casting and trading fish need no approval and cost only the small fee.

NEAR to collect

Top up coins

Coins buy bait and upgrades in the game. They only ever come from a top-up, and that NEAR goes into the prize vault.

You approve each top-up yourself. This is testnet NEAR: it is free and worth nothing.

Today's draw

Burn tickets to enter. A day's draw takes 500 tickets, 10 from one player, first come first served. When the day is over one ticket in five wins, and every winning ticket gets a hundredth of the prize: about five times what a ticket costs to make. What nobody wins stays in the vault for later days. The prize is 20% of the vault as it was when the day began.
